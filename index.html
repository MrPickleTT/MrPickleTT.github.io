Jarvis

A personal assistant used only by Trent Heller.

Jarvis is a private, self-hosted assistant that runs on Trent's own computer. It helps him keep track of his schedule, coursework deadlines, and email. It is not a public product, it has no other users, and it is not offered to anyone else.

With Trent's permission, Jarvis has read-only access to his own Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Drive so it can answer questions like "what's on my calendar tomorrow?"
